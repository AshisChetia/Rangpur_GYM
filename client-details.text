Your Rights as a Gym Member in Assam
Indian gym members have clear rights under the Consumer Protection Act 2019, which significantly strengthened consumer protections compared to the earlier 1986 Act. The Act prohibits unfair trade practices by gyms, Including misleading advertising about facilities or services, pressure sales tactics, unfair one-sided cancellation terms, and failure to disclose all applicable charges Including GST. The Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) has jurisdiction to investigate and penalise fitness businesses engaging in such practices.

If you experience issues with a gym membership in India – Including difficulty cancelling, unexpected charges, or facilities that do not match what was advertised – you have several remedies available. You can file a complaint at your local District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (for claims up to Rs 1 crore), the State Commission (Rs 1-10 crore), or the National Commission (above Rs 10 crore). The National Consumer Helpline at 1800-11-4000 is free, available in multiple languages, and provides guidance on how to file complaints effectively.
